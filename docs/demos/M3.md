# M3 demo: student learning

## Offline (pretend mode, no server)

1. Install the debug APK and sign in as a **student** (any number; pretend mode accepts any code).
2. **Home** shows the stats bar (streak, XP, hearts), the daily goal (0 of 50 XP) and a blue **Continue learning** card for "Parts of a cell". Tap **Start**.
3. In the lesson:
   - Tap an answer: **Check** turns on. Tap **Check**: a green "बहुत बढ़िया! Great! +10 XP" sheet, or a red "Not quite" one with the right answer, the reason in both languages and a lost heart.
   - **Match pairs:** tap a word on the left, then its partner on the right. Matched pairs fade; a wrong pair shakes red and costs no heart.
   - **Fill the formula:** tap O₂ into the blank.
   - Underlined words (e.g. माइटोकॉन्ड्रिया / Mitochondria) open the **term card**: tap Save, then close it.
   - **Listen** (tap: regional then English; long-press: English only). Pretend mode has no audio.
   - **Ask doubt** opens doubt chat about the question; type "Why does the moon shine?" to see the "not in your notes" answer.
   - Questions you got wrong come back at the end ("Let's try this one again").
4. The end screen shows XP and accuracy; the first lesson of the day shows the gold streak screen ("1 day streak!").
5. **Learn** tab: lesson 1 has a tick, lesson 2 is the big cream node, lesson 3 is locked (tap it: "Finish … first"). **Terms** opens the glossary: switch to Saved, and search "माइटो", "ಮೈಟೊ" or "mito".
6. **Profile → Daily goal:** pick 30, 50 or 100.
7. Get five answers wrong to see the **out of hearts** pause.

## Real (PC dev server)

1. Start `backend/scripts/dev_server.py` (add the Sarvam key to `backend/.env` for real voices).
2. In the app, set Profile → Server (testing) to the address the server prints, and sign in with a Supabase test number.
3. As a teacher, publish lesson 1 of a course to a class; as a student (a second test number), join the class with its key.
4. Play the lesson. XP, hearts and the streak come from the server; **Listen** speaks with Sarvam's voices (the second tap of the same text uses the cache); a doubt answer cites a page of the notes.
